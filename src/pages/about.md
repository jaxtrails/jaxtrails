## What you'll find here

**Trail Reviews** are honest write-ups of parks and the trails inside them. Parks get a review. Trails get a review and a rating of one to five blazes.

**Trail Thoughts** is personal writing: Reflections, Books, and Everything Else.

## Why blazes

A blaze is the painted mark that tells you you're still on the trail. Here, it's also the rating.
